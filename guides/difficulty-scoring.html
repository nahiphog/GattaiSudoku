How Gattai Sudoku Difficulty Scoring Works

Ratings describe the current walkthrough's solve path, using technique points and difficulty floors.

Generation checks that a puzzle has exactly one solution. The difficulty rating and walkthrough appear automatically below each generated result. To evaluate your own puzzle, enter it on Verify a solution, select Check uniqueness, then evaluate it once uniqueness is confirmed.

The evaluator records each placement or candidate-elimination step. Its rating describes the path this solver found; it does not prove that every human solver would need the same techniques or find the puzzle equally difficult.

The rating starts at Beginner, applies the technique floors below, then moves upward while the total exceeds the current tier's maximum. The thresholds are inclusive: 400 points alone does not move a puzzle to Easy.

  • Pointing, Claiming, and naked or hidden pairs and triples set a minimum of Medium.
  • Naked/Hidden Quad, X-Wing, Swordfish, Jellyfish, Skyscraper, 2-String Kite, W-Wing, XY-Wing, XYZ-Wing, Remote Pairs, and Phistomefel Ring are Hard-level techniques. One such step sets a minimum of Tricky; two or more set a minimum of Hard, even if the same technique repeats.
  • A Trial and error step contributes 10,000 points, so any walkthrough using it reaches Nightmare.

Technique order and search

The solver follows a fixed order, restarting from Full House after each successful step. It does not choose the lowest point value from every possible deduction.

The order is Full House, Naked Single, Hidden Single, Naked Pair, Hidden Pair, Pointing/Claiming, Naked Triple, Hidden Triple, Naked Quad, Hidden Quad, basic fish (X-Wing, Swordfish, Jellyfish), Skyscraper, 2-String Kite, W-Wing, XY-Wing, XYZ-Wing, Remote Pairs, and Phistomefel Ring.

If these techniques cannot make progress, the walkthrough fills a cell from the verified complete solution and labels that step Trial and error. It then resumes the named techniques. The walkthrough does not display the uniqueness search's branching process. Forcing Chains, Colouring, Empty Rectangle, and Almost Locked Sets are not currently implemented as named walkthrough techniques.

Generation and difficulty are separate

Digging removes single clues or rotational pairs while preserving uniqueness. Build a puzzle adds individual clues while respecting your required-clue and required-blank cells. Neither method targets a selected difficulty tier or guarantees that the named logical techniques will finish the puzzle.

Completing an unrestricted digging pass produces a puzzle with no further removable single clue or rotational pair, according to the chosen mode. A time cap, Halt, or a Stop at givens limit can leave additional removable clues. This minimality is relative to the chosen removal mode; it is not a proof of the smallest possible clue count.
