HOW TO PLAY

Difficulty guide

Fill each 9×9 grid so every row, column, and 3×3 house contains 1–9 exactly once. The shared six-by-six area obeys both grids at once.

Every puzzle is dug in rotational clue pairs: the selected clue and its 180° partner in one grid are removed together only when the Gattai stays uniquely solvable by the supported logic. Wednesday and Thursday retain a subset step; Friday through Sunday retain at least one Fish, Single-Digit Pattern, or Wing step.
