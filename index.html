HOW TO PLAY

Difficulty guide

Fill each 9×9 grid so every row, column, and 3×3 house contains 1–9 exactly once. The shared six-by-six area obeys both grids at once.

Beginner · 0–249
Full House and Naked Single.
Easy · 250–599
Everything in Beginner, plus Hidden Single. Both current puzzles are Easy (500).
Intermediate · 600–999
Everything in Beginner and Easy, plus Locked Pair, Locked Triple, Pointing, and Claiming.
Advanced · 1000+
Everything in all earlier bands, plus multiple interacting candidate patterns and advanced eliminations.
