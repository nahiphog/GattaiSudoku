HOW TO PLAY

Difficulty guide

Fill each 9×9 grid so every row, column, and 3×3 house contains 1–9 exactly once. The shared six-by-six area obeys both grids at once.

Beginner · 0–249
Full House and Naked Single.
Easy · 250–599
Full House, Naked Single, and Hidden Single. Both current puzzles are Easy (500).
Intermediate · 600–999
Locked Pair, Locked Triple, Pointing, and Claiming.
Advanced · 1000+
Multiple interacting candidate patterns and advanced eliminations.
