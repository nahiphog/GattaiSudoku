HOW TO PLAY

Difficulty guide

Fill each 9×9 grid so every row, column, and 3×3 house contains 1–9 exactly once. The shared six-by-six area obeys both grids at once.

Beginner · ≤400
Full House and Naked Single.
Easy · ≤800
Everything in Beginner, plus Hidden Single.
Medium · ≤1000
Everything in Easy, plus intersections and Naked or Hidden Pairs and Triples.
Tricky · ≤1150
Everything in Medium, with additional bookkeeping-heavy patterns.
Hard · ≤1600
Everything in Tricky, plus Basic Fish such as X-Wing, Finned Fish, Complex Fish, and other advanced eliminations.
Unfair, Extreme, Nightmare
Each subsequent band includes all techniques from every earlier band, then adds progressively more complex chains, colouring, ALSs, and last-resort patterns.

Built for deliberate solving · Method names and ratings follow sudokUI.
